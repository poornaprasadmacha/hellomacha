    Why Rich People Sometimes Use Debt
  </h2>

  <p className="mb-6 text-gray-700">
    Wealthy individuals and business owners often have assets, cash, and investments. When they need capital to expand, they don't automatically sell their assets. Selling investments reduces ownership and liquidity. Borrowing allows them to keep assets while accessing capital.
  </p>

  <p className="mb-6 text-gray-700 font-semibold">
    This does not mean borrowing is automatically better. The loan has a cost and repayment risk. The strategy only works if the borrower can manage that risk.
  </p>
