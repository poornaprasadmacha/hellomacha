<div className="not-prose font-sans text-gray-800 leading-relaxed bg-transparent">

<p className="mb-6 text-lg text-gray-800 leading-relaxed">A headline about <strong>UPI charges above ₹2,000</strong> can sound like every payment is about to cost more. The key is to separate a payment between two people from a payment to a merchant, then identify who the MDR applies to.</p>

<p className="mb-6 text-lg text-gray-800 leading-relaxed">The framework discussed here mentions <strong>0.4% MDR</strong>, a <strong>₹2,000 threshold</strong> and a <strong>₹300 cap</strong>. Those figures do not mean that every UPI user is charged a new fee. The transaction type and the merchant category matter.</p>

<p className="mb-6 text-lg text-gray-800 leading-relaxed">The actual picture is more specific.</p>

<div className="bg-red-50 border border-red-100 p-6 my-8">
<p className="mb-4 text-lg text-gray-800 leading-relaxed">The short answer:</p>
<p className="mb-0 text-lg text-gray-900 font-normal"><strong>Person-to-person UPI payments remain free. Merchant payments up to ₹2,000 remain free of MDR. For specified merchant payments above ₹2,000, MDR can apply, but the customer is not supposed to pay that MDR.</strong></p>
</div>

<p className="mb-6 text-lg text-gray-800 leading-relaxed">That last sentence is the part many short social-media posts leave out.</p>

<p className="mb-8 text-lg text-gray-900 font-normal">Here is the practical version: what changes, what remains zero MDR, and what a customer should do if a payment screen or receipt seems to show a charge.</p>

<h2 className="text-2xl md:text-3xl font-bold text-gray-900 mt-16 mb-6 tracking-tight !no-underline">What Is Actually Changing in October 2026?</h2>

<p className="mb-6 text-lg text-gray-800 leading-relaxed">The revised UPI MDR framework is scheduled to take effect from <strong>15 October 2026</strong>.</p>

<p className="mb-6 text-lg text-gray-800 leading-relaxed">For specified <strong>person-to-merchant (P2M)</strong> transactions above ₹2,000, the standard MDR is <strong>0.4%</strong>. For transactions of ₹75,000 and above, the standard MDR is capped at <strong>₹300 per transaction</strong>.</p>

<p className="mb-6 text-lg text-gray-800 leading-relaxed">But several important parts of UPI remain free:</p>

<ul className="list-disc list-inside space-y-3 mb-8 text-lg text-gray-800">
<li>Person-to-person payments remain free regardless of amount.</li>
<li>Merchant payments up to ₹2,000 remain free of MDR.</li>
<li>Eligible small merchants covered by the zero-MDR P2PM framework remain protected.</li>
<li>Customers are not supposed to pay MDR.</li>
</ul>

<p className="mb-8 text-lg text-gray-900 font-normal">So this is not a simple story of "UPI is now chargeable." It is a change to the way certain merchant payments are handled.</p>

<h2 className="text-2xl md:text-3xl font-bold text-gray-900 mt-16 mb-6 tracking-tight !no-underline">The ₹2,000 Rule Is Not for Every UPI Payment</h2>

<p className="mb-6 text-lg text-gray-800 leading-relaxed">This is the first thing to understand.</p>

<div className="grid md:grid-cols-2 gap-6 mb-10">
<div className="bg-gray-50 border border-gray-200 p-6">
<h3 className="text-xl md:text-2xl font-semibold text-gray-900 mt-2 mb-4 tracking-tight !no-underline">P2P — Person to Person</h3>
<p className="mb-4 text-lg text-gray-800 leading-relaxed">You send money to another person.</p>
<p className="mb-0 text-lg text-gray-900 font-normal"><strong>Example:</strong> You send ₹10,000 to your brother.</p>
</div>
<div className="bg-gray-50 border border-gray-200 p-6">
<h3 className="text-xl md:text-2xl font-semibold text-gray-900 mt-2 mb-4 tracking-tight !no-underline">P2M — Person to Merchant</h3>
<p className="mb-4 text-lg text-gray-800 leading-relaxed">You pay a shop, restaurant, business, or other merchant.</p>
<p className="mb-0 text-lg text-gray-900 font-normal"><strong>Example:</strong> You pay a shop ₹10,000.</p>
</div>
</div>

<p className="mb-6 text-lg text-gray-800 leading-relaxed">If you send ₹10,000 to your brother, the new MDR does not apply. If you pay a merchant ₹10,000, the merchant-side MDR framework may apply.</p>

<p className="mb-8 text-lg text-gray-900 font-normal">So whenever you hear, <em>"UPI above ₹2,000 will be charged,"</em> ask one more question: <strong>"Is this P2P or P2M?"</strong></p>

<h2 className="text-2xl md:text-3xl font-bold text-gray-900 mt-16 mb-6 tracking-tight !no-underline">Will Customers Have to Pay the 0.4%?</h2>

<p className="mb-6 text-lg text-gray-800 leading-relaxed"><strong>No, not as the new MDR.</strong></p>

<p className="mb-6 text-lg text-gray-800 leading-relaxed">The government has clarified that MDR is a charge within the merchant payment ecosystem, not a customer UPI usage fee. Banks have been advised to ensure that merchants do not pass MDR on to customers, and UPI application providers are not allowed to impose platform fees or hidden charges under this framework.</p>

<div className="bg-gray-50 border border-gray-200 p-6 my-8">
<p className="mb-3 text-lg text-gray-900 font-normal"><strong>Imagine a ₹10,000 shop purchase:</strong></p>
<p className="mb-3 text-lg text-gray-800 leading-relaxed">Purchase value: ₹10,000</p>
<p className="mb-3 text-lg text-gray-800 leading-relaxed">0.4% MDR calculation: ₹40</p>
<p className="mb-0 text-lg text-gray-900 font-normal"><strong>Customer's new MDR fee: ₹0</strong></p>
</div>

<p className="mb-8 text-lg text-gray-800 leading-relaxed">The ₹40 is the merchant-side MDR calculation within the applicable payment framework. It is not another ₹40 that the customer is supposed to add to the bill.</p>

<p className="mb-6 text-lg text-gray-800 leading-relaxed">At ₹75,000, 0.4% equals ₹300. Above that, the cap matters. So a ₹1 lakh payment does not create ₹400 of standard MDR; the cap keeps it at ₹300.</p>

<p className="mb-8 text-lg text-gray-900 font-normal">The cap is one reason you should not simply multiply every large UPI payment by 0.4% and assume that is the final MDR.</p>

<h2 className="text-2xl md:text-3xl font-bold text-gray-900 mt-16 mb-6 tracking-tight !no-underline">What Happens at Exactly ₹2,000?</h2>

<p className="mb-6 text-lg text-gray-800 leading-relaxed">The wording is important: merchant payments <strong>up to ₹2,000</strong> remain free of MDR.</p>

<div className="bg-red-50 border border-red-100 p-6 my-8">
<p className="mb-3 text-lg text-gray-900 font-normal"><strong>₹2,000 merchant payment:</strong> zero MDR</p>
<p className="mb-0 text-lg text-gray-900 font-normal"><strong>₹2,001 specified merchant payment:</strong> 0.4% MDR calculation</p>
</div>

<p className="mb-8 text-lg text-gray-800 leading-relaxed">That one-rupee difference is why the ₹2,000 threshold has become such a popular search query.</p>

<h2 className="text-2xl md:text-3xl font-bold text-gray-900 mt-16 mb-6 tracking-tight !no-underline">What About Small Shops and Street Vendors?</h2>

<p className="mb-6 text-lg text-gray-800 leading-relaxed">There is an important zero-MDR provision for small merchants.</p>

<p className="mb-6 text-lg text-gray-800 leading-relaxed">Small merchants, including street vendors, receiving up to <strong>₹1 lakh per month through UPI QR codes</strong> under the P2PM category continue to receive zero-MDR treatment on their transactions, subject to the applicable conditions.</p>

<p className="mb-8 text-lg text-gray-900 font-normal">So a small neighbourhood shop should not automatically assume that every payment above ₹2,000 will be charged at 0.4%.</p>

<h2 className="text-2xl md:text-3xl font-bold text-gray-900 mt-16 mb-6 tracking-tight !no-underline">What About Railways, Fuel, Insurance and Telecom?</h2>

<p className="mb-6 text-lg text-gray-800 leading-relaxed">Some essential and thin-margin sectors have a different rate.</p>

<p className="mb-6 text-lg text-gray-800 leading-relaxed">For applicable transactions above ₹2,000 in sectors such as <strong>railways, telecommunications, insurance, fuel and agricultural inputs</strong>, the framework specifies a flat <strong>₹5 MDR per transaction</strong>.</p>

<p className="mb-8 text-lg text-gray-800 leading-relaxed">So the answer to "What is the UPI charge?" depends on what kind of transaction you are talking about. The standard 0.4% rate is not the answer for every category.</p>

<h2 className="text-2xl md:text-3xl font-bold text-gray-900 mt-16 mb-6 tracking-tight !no-underline">What About Mutual Funds and Stockbrokers?</h2>

<p className="mb-6 text-lg text-gray-800 leading-relaxed">Capital-market related payments have another specified rate.</p>

<p className="mb-6 text-lg text-gray-800 leading-relaxed">Payments relating to <strong>mutual funds, securities, stockbrokers and dealers</strong> attract an MDR of <strong>0.02%</strong>, capped at ₹300 per transaction.</p>

<div className="bg-gray-50 border border-gray-200 p-6 my-8">
<p className="mb-0 text-lg text-gray-800 leading-relaxed">This is why the phrase <em>"UPI charges are 0.4%"</em> is too broad. The transaction category matters.</p>
</div>

<h2 className="text-2xl md:text-3xl font-bold text-gray-900 mt-16 mb-6 tracking-tight !no-underline">Why Is MDR Being Introduced?</h2>

<p className="mb-6 text-lg text-gray-800 leading-relaxed">UPI has grown far beyond a simple way to split a restaurant bill. It is now a major part of India's digital payment infrastructure.</p>

<p className="mb-6 text-lg text-gray-800 leading-relaxed">The government says the revised framework is intended to support the <strong>long-term sustainability of UPI</strong> while protecting individuals and small merchants from additional charges.</p>

<p className="mb-6 text-lg text-gray-800 leading-relaxed">That means the policy is trying to address the economics of running a very large payment network without turning everyday person-to-person payments into a paid service.</p>

<p className="mb-8 text-lg text-gray-900 font-normal">The actual impact on individual businesses will depend on their payment mix and merchant category.</p>

<h2 className="text-2xl md:text-3xl font-bold text-gray-900 mt-16 mb-6 tracking-tight !no-underline">How Big Is UPI Now?</h2>

<p className="mb-6 text-lg text-gray-800 leading-relaxed">The scale of UPI helps explain why even a small change attracts so much attention.</p>

<p className="mb-6 text-lg text-gray-800 leading-relaxed">NPCI's August 2026 statistics show <strong>24,508.96 million UPI transactions</strong> in the month, worth about <strong>₹29.82 lakh crore</strong>.</p>

<p className="mb-8 text-lg text-gray-800 leading-relaxed">When a network processes that many payments, even a small change to the way merchant transactions are funded can affect banks, payment providers and businesses across the country.</p>

<h2 className="text-2xl md:text-3xl font-bold text-gray-900 mt-16 mb-6 tracking-tight !no-underline">How Much of UPI Is Actually Affected?</h2>

<p className="mb-6 text-lg text-gray-800 leading-relaxed">The Ministry of Finance says MDR will apply to only about <strong>4% of merchant transactions</strong>. That means approximately <strong>96% of P2M transactions will remain unaffected</strong>.</p>

<div className="bg-gray-50 border border-gray-200 p-6 my-8">
<p className="mb-3 text-lg text-gray-900 font-normal"><strong>About 96%</strong> of P2M transactions → remain unaffected</p>
<p className="mb-0 text-lg text-gray-900 font-normal"><strong>About 4%</strong> → fall into the MDR-affected group</p>
</div>

<p className="mb-8 text-lg text-gray-800 leading-relaxed">That does not mean every merchant will have the same cost. The merchant's transaction size, category and eligibility for zero-MDR treatment all matter.</p>

<h2 className="text-2xl md:text-3xl font-bold text-gray-900 mt-16 mb-6 tracking-tight !no-underline">UPI Limit and UPI Charge Are Not the Same Thing</h2>

<p className="mb-6 text-lg text-gray-800 leading-relaxed">You may also see searches about UPI daily transaction limits at the same time as searches about UPI charges. These are separate issues.</p>

<p className="mb-6 text-lg text-gray-800 leading-relaxed"><strong>A limit tells you how much you can transact. A charge tells you how much you have to pay.</strong></p>

<p className="mb-6 text-lg text-gray-800 leading-relaxed">The government says daily limits prescribed by banks and NPCI, generally ranging from ₹1 lakh to ₹5 lakh depending on the transaction category, are security and risk-management safeguards. They are not charging thresholds.</p>

<p className="mb-8 text-lg text-gray-800 leading-relaxed">So a ₹1 lakh daily limit does not mean UPI starts charging you after ₹1 lakh.</p>

<h2 className="text-2xl md:text-3xl font-bold text-gray-900 mt-16 mb-6 tracking-tight !no-underline">What About the Old GST-on-UPI Rumour?</h2>

<p className="mb-6 text-lg text-gray-800 leading-relaxed">The ₹2,000 number has appeared in UPI rumours before.</p>

<p className="mb-6 text-lg text-gray-800 leading-relaxed">In April 2025, the government said claims that it was considering GST on UPI transactions above ₹2,000 were false and without basis at that time.</p>

<p className="mb-6 text-lg text-gray-800 leading-relaxed">The 2026 MDR framework is different. It is a merchant payment ecosystem charge for specified transactions, while customers are not supposed to pay MDR.</p>

<div className="bg-red-50 border border-red-100 p-6 my-8">
<p className="mb-0 text-lg text-gray-900 font-normal"><strong>Don't mix the old GST rumour with the new MDR framework.</strong> They are two different issues.</p>
</div>

<h2 className="text-2xl md:text-3xl font-bold text-gray-900 mt-16 mb-6 tracking-tight !no-underline">What Does This Mean for You?</h2>

<p className="mb-6 text-lg text-gray-800 leading-relaxed">For most everyday UPI users, there is no reason to suddenly change how you use UPI because of the new MDR framework.</p>

<p className="mb-6 text-lg text-gray-800 leading-relaxed">If you send money to family, split a bill with friends, repay someone, or make ordinary payments to small merchants, the framework does not turn those activities into a monthly paid UPI service.</p>

<p className="mb-8 text-lg text-gray-900 font-normal">The most useful habit is simply to understand whether a payment is P2P or P2M and, for merchants, whether a special category applies.</p>

<h2 className="text-2xl md:text-3xl font-bold text-gray-900 mt-16 mb-6 tracking-tight !no-underline">What Does This Mean for Merchants?</h2>

<p className="mb-6 text-lg text-gray-800 leading-relaxed">Merchants have more reason to look at the details.</p>

<p className="mb-6 text-lg text-gray-800 leading-relaxed">A business should know:</p>

<ul className="list-disc list-inside space-y-3 mb-8 text-lg text-gray-800">
<li>How much of its UPI volume is above ₹2,000.</li>
<li>Whether it falls under the small-merchant zero-MDR framework.</li>
<li>Whether a special sector rate applies.</li>
<li>How its acquiring bank or payment provider will show the MDR in settlement.</li>
</ul>

<p className="mb-8 text-lg text-gray-900 font-normal">A shop receiving hundreds of ₹500 payments has a very different exposure from a business receiving fewer but much larger UPI payments.</p>

<p className="mb-8 text-lg text-gray-800 leading-relaxed">No. MDR is a merchant payment ecosystem charge. It is not the same thing as a government tax.</p>

<h2 className="text-2xl md:text-3xl font-bold text-gray-900 mt-16 mb-6 tracking-tight !no-underline">Final Thoughts</h2>

<p className="mb-6 text-lg text-gray-800 leading-relaxed">The new UPI rules are easier to understand once you stop treating the ₹2,000 number as a fee threshold for every person using UPI.</p>

<p className="mb-6 text-lg text-gray-800 leading-relaxed">It is mainly a threshold within the merchant payment framework.</p>

<blockquote className="text-xl md:text-2xl font-serif italic text-gray-900 border-l-4 border-red-700 pl-6 my-12 leading-relaxed">P2P remains free. Merchant payments up to ₹2,000 remain zero MDR. Specified merchant payments above ₹2,000 can attract MDR. Customers are not supposed to pay that MDR.</blockquote>

<p className="mb-6 text-lg text-gray-800 leading-relaxed">There are also separate provisions for small merchants, essential sectors and capital-market payments. So the right question is not simply <em>"Are UPI charges starting?"</em></p>

<p className="mb-8 text-lg text-gray-900 font-normal"><strong>The better question is: "Which UPI transaction are we talking about, and who is actually responsible for the MDR?"</strong></p>
