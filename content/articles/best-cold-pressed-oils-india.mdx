  <h2 className="text-2xl md:text-3xl font-bold text-gray-900 mt-16 mb-6 tracking-tight !no-underline">
    The Toxic Truth: What Are Refined Oils & Why Are They Dangerous?
  </h2>

  <p className="mb-6 text-lg text-gray-800 leading-relaxed">
    To understand why you should immediately remove refined oils from your pantry, you need to understand how they are manufactured. The traditional cold-pressing method yields less oil from the seed. To maximize profits, the commercial food industry developed the "refining" process to squeeze every absolute drop of oil out of the raw material using severe chemical intervention.
  </p>

  <ul className="list-disc list-inside space-y-3 mb-8 text-lg text-gray-800">
    <li><strong>Chemical Solvent Extraction:</strong> Leftover seed pulp is washed with a petroleum derivative called Hexane to dissolve the remaining oil. While evaporated later, dangerous trace amounts often remain.</li>
    <li><strong>Acid Degumming & Neutralization:</strong> The crude oil is treated with phosphoric acid and sodium hydroxide (caustic soda) to remove natural gums. This process creates a soap-like byproduct that must be chemically washed out.</li>
    <li><strong>High-Heat Deodorization (The Killer Step):</strong> The oil is heated to extreme temperatures (reaching 230°C–260°C) to remove its natural smell and color. This extreme heat breaks down the fatty acid chains, forcing the creation of dangerous <em>trans fats</em> and <em>glycidyl esters</em>—compounds heavily linked to cardiovascular diseases, arterial blockages, and chronic body inflammation.</li>
  </ul>

  <p className="mb-6 text-lg text-gray-800 leading-relaxed">
    By the time a refined oil reaches your cooking pan, up to 90% of its Vitamin E, natural antioxidants, and polyphenols have been entirely destroyed.
  </p>

  <p className="mb-6 text-lg text-gray-800 leading-relaxed">

  <p className="mb-6 text-lg text-gray-800 leading-relaxed">
