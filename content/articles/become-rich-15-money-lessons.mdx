  <p className="mb-6 text-lg text-gray-800 leading-relaxed">If your essential bills and debt payments allow it, schedule a manageable transfer to savings soon after payday. <strong>Save first, then plan spending around what remains.</strong> The amount should be sustainable rather than chosen to impress anyone.</p>
