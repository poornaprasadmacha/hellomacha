  <p className="mb-6 text-lg text-gray-800 leading-relaxed">Traditional LED TVs rely on white backlight filtering through liquid crystal layers, which can cause light bleed and muted colors. QLED technology places a film layer of nano-sized Quantum Dots in front of the backlight.
