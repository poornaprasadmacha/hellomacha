  <h2 id="what-is-qled" className="text-2xl md:text-3xl font-bold text-gray-900 mt-16 mb-6 tracking-tight !no-underline">What is QLED Technology & Why Choose It?</h2>
  <p className="mb-6 text-lg text-gray-800 leading-relaxed">A conventional LCD television uses a backlight and colour filters; a QLED set adds a quantum-dot layer that helps produce a wider range of saturated colours. QLED is not the same as OLED: QLED sets still rely on a backlight, while OLED pixels emit their own light. Local dimming, panel type, brightness and processing differ across products, so don't assume every QLED has the same contrast or viewing angles.</p>

  <h2 className="text-2xl md:text-3xl font-bold text-gray-900 mt-16 mb-6 tracking-tight !no-underline">How to compare QLED televisions in a real room</h2>
  <p className="mb-6 text-lg text-gray-800 leading-relaxed">Start with placement. Measure the viewing distance, width of the stand or wall, and the height at which the screen will sit. A 55-inch screen can feel immersive from a sofa but overwhelm a small bedroom; a 32-inch model can be a practical second-room set but is not a substitute for a large 4K display if you sit far away. Consider where windows and lamps are positioned, because reflections can make a bright panel harder to watch than its specification suggests.</p>
  <p className="mb-6 text-lg text-gray-800 leading-relaxed">Resolution should match the size and content. The TCL, Hisense and Toshiba picks listed here are 4K models; the two VW 32-inch sets are HD Ready. HD Ready can be perfectly serviceable for a compact room, ordinary television channels or a secondary screen, but it contains less detail than 4K. Streaming services and broadcast channels also vary in resolution, so a 4K TV cannot turn every low-resolution source into native 4K. Upscaling can make content look cleaner, but it cannot restore detail that was never in the source.</p>
  <p className="mb-6 text-lg text-gray-800 leading-relaxed">For sport, look for steady motion and a picture mode that suits the content. Processing can reduce judder or sharpen a broadcast, but aggressive motion smoothing may make a film look unnatural. Try the available modes and keep the option to turn processing down. A feature such as AI Sports Mode is a processing aid, not a guarantee that every broadcast will look better; the result depends on the source signal and personal preference.</p>
  <p className="mb-6 text-lg text-gray-800 leading-relaxed">Gaming buyers should inspect the input specifications carefully. VRR and ALLM can be useful with compatible consoles or PCs, but check which HDMI ports support each function and at what resolution and refresh rate. eARC is useful for connecting an audio system, but it does not mean every port supports every gaming mode. If the TV will mostly be used for streaming, app availability and a responsive interface are likely to matter more than an advanced gaming feature you never enable.</p>
  <p className="mb-6 text-lg text-gray-800 leading-relaxed">Think about the software you use. Google TV, VIDAA and Android TV offer different interfaces and app catalogues; confirm that your preferred streaming apps, regional services and voice-control options are available in your country and on the specific model. A built-in voice assistant can make search easier, but check microphone and privacy settings if you do not want voice features enabled. Also review software-update support where the manufacturer publishes it, since an operating system is part of the ownership experience.</p>
  <p className="mb-6 text-lg text-gray-800 leading-relaxed">Built-in sound is another point where the model's use matters more than a number on a box. The Hisense listing highlights 48W speakers, while the TCL, Toshiba and VW cards list their own audio configurations. Wattage alone does not describe dialogue clarity, bass or room-filling performance. If you plan to add a soundbar, confirm the connection you need—such as HDMI eARC or optical audio—and leave enough space for it under the screen.</p>
  <p className="mb-6 text-lg text-gray-800 leading-relaxed">Before ordering, confirm the model suffix, resolution, warranty, installation process and return policy on the seller page. Product photos can show a related model, and sale prices or bank discounts can change after publication. Make sure a card offer applies to your card and that an EMI plan does not add a fee that offsets the discount. Review delivery access, wall-mount requirements and installation charges before checkout.</p>
  <p className="mb-6 text-lg text-gray-800 leading-relaxed">For a quick decision, the TCL 55T6D offers a 55-inch 4K QLED route with Google TV and listed Dolby formats; Hisense 55E75Q highlights built-in sound and gaming features; Toshiba 55M450RP focuses on REGZA processing; and the VW models prioritise compact size. Compare those trade-offs against your room and budget. Treat linked offers as a place to check current details, not as a promise that a price or promotion will remain unchanged.</p>
