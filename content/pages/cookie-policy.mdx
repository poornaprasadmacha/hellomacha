# Cookie Policy

**Last Updated:** September 20, 2026

This Cookie Policy explains how HelloMacha uses cookies and similar technologies when you visit the website.

## What cookies are

Cookies are small text files stored by your browser. They help websites remember preferences, understand usage, and provide certain features.

## How HelloMacha uses cookies

We may use essential storage for preferences such as cookie consent. If you accept non-essential cookies, Google Analytics may be used to understand traffic and improve the website. Advertising partners may also use cookies when advertising is enabled.

## Your choices

You can reject non-essential cookies using the cookie preference notice. You can also delete or block cookies through your browser settings. Blocking cookies may affect some features.

## Third parties

Analytics and advertising providers may process information according to their own privacy policies. HelloMacha does not sell personal information.
