# Cookie Policy

**Last Updated:** September 20, 2026

This Cookie Policy explains how HelloMacha uses cookies and similar technologies when you visit the website.

## What cookies are

Cookies are small text files stored by your browser. They help websites remember preferences, understand usage, and provide certain features.

## How HelloMacha uses cookies

We may use essential storage for preferences such as cookie consent. If you accept non-essential cookies, Google Analytics may be used to understand traffic and improve the website. Advertising partners may also use cookies when advertising is enabled.

## Types of storage we may use

### Essential storage

This storage supports basic site functions, such as remembering whether you have made a cookie choice. These items are used because the site cannot reliably provide the related preference without them.

### Analytics storage

If enabled, analytics tools help us understand general activity such as page visits, approximate device information, referral sources, and broad usage patterns. We use this information to improve navigation and content. Analytics data is not intended to identify you personally.

### Advertising storage

When advertising is enabled, advertising partners may use cookies or similar technologies to measure ad performance, limit repeated ads, or show more relevant advertising. The availability and behaviour of these technologies can change when our advertising providers change their settings.

## How long cookies last

Some cookies expire when you close your browser. Others remain for a limited period so that a preference can be remembered on a later visit. The duration depends on the purpose of the cookie and the provider that sets it.

## Managing your choices

You can change your choice by deleting site data in your browser and revisiting HelloMacha. You can also manage permissions through your browser's privacy settings. A browser-level block may apply to other websites as well, and deleting cookies may remove saved preferences.

This policy explains the website's cookie practices and does not replace the privacy notices of third-party services. For information about personal data more generally, please read our Privacy Policy.

## Your choices

You can reject non-essential cookies using the cookie preference notice. You can also delete or block cookies through your browser settings. Blocking cookies may affect some features.

## Third parties

Analytics and advertising providers may process information according to their own privacy policies. HelloMacha does not sell personal information.
