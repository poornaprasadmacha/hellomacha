# Editorial Policy

HelloMacha publishes practical guides about personal finance, technology, business, and everyday products. Our goal is to make complicated decisions easier to understand without presenting general information as personal advice.

## Research and sourcing

We aim to use primary and official sources wherever possible, including government departments, regulators, company documentation, product manuals, and official scheme pages. Readers should check the linked source and confirm current rules, prices, rates, and eligibility before acting.

Articles may also use reputable secondary sources when they add context, comparisons, or historical background. We try to distinguish reported facts from our interpretation, label estimates, and show the assumptions behind examples and calculators. A source is not treated as an endorsement, and a link may be updated or replaced when an official page changes.

## How we evaluate products and services

Product guides consider the factors that matter to the intended use case, such as price, features, durability, efficiency, warranty, availability, and total ownership cost. We do not claim that one choice is best for every reader. Rankings and recommendations may change when specifications, prices, policies, or market alternatives change.

## Financial content

Our financial articles and calculators are educational. They do not create an adviser-client relationship, guarantee returns, or recommend a product for every reader. Investment outcomes depend on risk, time horizon, taxes, fees, market conditions, and personal circumstances.

## Reviews and affiliate links

When an article includes affiliate links, HelloMacha may earn a commission if a reader purchases through a link. This does not change the price paid by the reader. Commercial relationships do not guarantee a positive review or ranking.

## Updates and corrections

We update articles when important facts, rules, prices, product specifications, or calculations change. If you find an error, contact **team.hellomacha@gmail.com** with the article URL and the correction. We will review credible reports and correct material errors as soon as practical.

Corrections are made in the article when a factual error could affect a reader's understanding or decision. We may also add an updated date or a short note when a significant change affects the conclusion. Minor spelling or formatting fixes may not receive a separate notice.

## Use of AI and automation

Tools may assist with outlining, formatting, research organisation, or checking calculations. Published claims, conclusions, and material edits remain subject to human review. We do not treat automatically generated text as a substitute for checking an official source or testing a calculation.

## Editorial independence

The editorial team decides topics, conclusions, and recommendations independently. Sponsored or commercial relationships will be identified when applicable.

## Contact

For corrections, source suggestions, or editorial questions, email **team.hellomacha@gmail.com**.
